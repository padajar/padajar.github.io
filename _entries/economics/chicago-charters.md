title: "From Cubs to Bears: The Evolution of Charter Effectiveness in Chicago"
summary: "How effective are charters? How has that effectiveness changed over time, and why?"
panels:
  - label: "abstract"
    body: |
      This paper estimates how the effectiveness of Chicago charter schools has changed between 2009 and 2025. Using random variation from admissions lotteries, I validate earlier research showing high returns to attending Chicago charter schools. I also show effectiveness decreased during the 2010s, but has rebounded in recent years. I explore determinants of these changes, including teacher qualifications, teacher turnover, leadership changes, curricular changes, and policy changes.
