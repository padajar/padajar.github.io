At the time I TA'ed, MIT Economics TAs were typically graduate students, and I've since learned that me being a TA helped pave the way for the multitude of MEng/undergrad TAs that exist now. While I know my TAing wasn't perfect (a few mistakes in office hours, imperfect recitation timing), I'd like to think I did a fairly good job in terms of helping students (overall rating 5.5/7), and I certainly learned much from that experience. The most fun I had here was getting to design recitations to introduce students to game theory, and creating problems that related to my research.
