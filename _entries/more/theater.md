summary: |
  I love musicals! Favorite ones I've seen in-person are Hadestown, Waitress, and Maybe Happy Ending. A few years ago, I started trying out the world of acting in them. My first show was Next Act 2023, *Remembering Her*, where I played Kevin, a wholesome, slightly empty-headed, Prius-driving highschooler who forgets his girlfriend, but eventually remembers (shyeah!). My biggest role to date was Michael in a production of *tick, tick... BOOM!* with KAST, which was an absolute joy. Through Club Drosselmeyer, I've gotten into the local Boston interactive theater scene, and have been involved with productions like [Romancing Jan: Love & Labor](https://www.romancingjan.com/live-events) (a Bridgerton-esque LARP) and [The Wedding Party](https://www.secretcityadventures.com/the-wedding-party/boston) (a wedding reception where absolutely nothing weird will happen), and getting to improvise in-character has been such a blast. A few things on the horizon, like [Carnavale Di Oscurità](https://www.incantrixproductions.com/carnevale-di-oscurita) — here's to many more shows!
