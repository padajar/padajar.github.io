summary: |
  Over the last year, I've gotten into Boston's interactive theater scene, with roles in  [Romancing Jan: Love & Labor](https://www.romancingjan.com/live-events) (a Bridgerton-esque LARP), [The Wedding Party](https://www.secretcityadventures.com/the-wedding-party/boston) (a wedding reception where absolutely nothing goes wrong), and [Blood Hunt](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/blood-hunt-a-comedy-vampire-bar-crawl-tickets-1998406042268) (a vampire-themed pub crawl). On the horizon is [Carnevale di Oscurità](https://www.incantrixproductions.com/carnevale-di-oscurita) (a circus set in 1907 with fantastical acts and mysteries to uncover) in February 2027. My first big show was Next Act 2023, *Remembering Her* by [Alan Zhu](https://www.alanwhyz.com/) where I played Kevin, a wholesome, slightly empty-headed, Prius-driving teen (shyeah!) who has somehow forgotten his girlfriend. My biggest role to date was Michael in a production of *tick, tick... BOOM!* with KAST, which was an absolute joy. Also love musicals --- some select favorites are Operation Mincemeat, Hadestown, Waitress, and Maybe Happy Ending!
